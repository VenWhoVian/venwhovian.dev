About

Hey, I'm Ven.

I'm a developer who mostly builds mods. My own project is Ringbound — a Sonic the Hedgehog mod I'm building because I wanted Sonic done properly: speed you have to earn, momentum you have to keep, rings that matter.

Alongside that I work with the Gravestone Team on the Gravestone add-on pack line — Core, Herotime and Quirk Odyssey — where a lot of the work is making a big shared foundation hold up under everything the pack throws at it.

Everything ships publicly. Progress, previews and the occasional broken build all land in the Discord first.
